About Us

Established in 2010 by Chef Doris Ameh (Certified), DorisA Kitchen & Confectionery is a trusted culinary brand dedicated to providing delicious meals and exceptional services. Our passion is delivering quality food experiences through Nigerian delicacies, continental dishes, confectioneries and event catering services.

From intimate gatherings to large celebrations, we ensure every meal is prepared with excellence and served with love.
